Station kind colour

Six kinds need six colours: water level, rainfall, siren, flood gauge, camera, several sensors. The repository owner set the rule on 2026-08-26 — the whole hue range, and a chroma that separates a kind from the functional set. This page measures that rather than eyeballs it.

Every hex below is derived at load, in OKLCh. Nothing on this page was typed by hand. The page then fetches css/base.css and checks what it derived against what the app holds, so a palette here cannot drift from the palette that ships.
